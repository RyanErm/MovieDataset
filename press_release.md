# Headline


## Are you tired of finding movies on your own? Use Netflix's new recommendation algorithm to find your favorite movies and keep watching on the platform.


## Problem Statement  Netflix uses a reccomendation system to provide new content for users to watch. While this is beneficial to customers, as they find shows that they like, this is also beneficial to Netflix as users stay engaged on the platform for longer and might reccomend it to friends. However, current reccomendations are too singular and users feel that they are not being reccomended a variety of content. Poor reccomendations might give users negative feelings towards Netlifx, and might even make them more prone to cancelling their subscription. Additionally, with many new streaming services popping up, there is increased competition for Netflix and incentive to improve their program. Please refer to the graph below to view how competitive the market for streaming services is. This leads to the current, specific problem: How can Netflix's content reccomendation algorithm be improved to keep users engaged on the platform?



## Solution Description Our solution! A new algorithm for Netflix should satisfies customers concerns that they are not being provided a wide variety of content to watch. To that end, our algorithm will not only draw from the users actions, but will also reccomend shows outside of their "comfort zone" (usual watching habits). For example, a user who has been watching many documentaries on war could be reccomended a fictional political thriller. By providing new generes of content, users hopefully find whole new fields of content to watch, and remain engaged on Netflix's platform.
